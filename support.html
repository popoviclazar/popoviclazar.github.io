Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap or hold LEFT to lower the left end of the platform, or RIGHT to lower the right end. Keep the falling objects on the platform. The run ends when an object falls off or the platform tips over.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button at the top of the game screen. Switching to another tab or leaving the app also pauses your run. Return to Play and tap Resume.

How do I get coins and skins?

You earn coins at the end of every run. Open the Skins tab to unlock and choose a platform style. Skins only change the look of the platform. There are no in-app purchases.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep the game free. A banner appears at the bottom of the screen, and sometimes a full-screen ad appears before a new run starts. Ads never appear in the middle of a run.

How do I change ad and tracking settings?

To change tracking permission, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, open the game's Settings tab and tap Ad privacy options.

Why can't I see the leaderboard?

The leaderboard uses Apple Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app and make sure you are online. The game works fully without Game Center.

I lost my progress after reinstalling

Progress is stored only on your iPhone, so deleting the app also deletes your coins, skins and scores. Game Center scores you submitted stay on the leaderboard.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Gameplay and your local records work without internet. Ads and Game Center need a connection.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Open the Settings tab in the game and switch off Sound effects or Haptics. The iPhone silent switch also mutes game sounds.
