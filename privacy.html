Privacy policy

What information Drop & Balance and its ad provider use, and the choices you have.

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Lazar PopovićIndependent developer and publisher of Drop & Balance, Montenegro
Email: popoviclazar65@gmail.com

Summary

Drop & Balance does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and it has no account system. Your game progress stays on your iPhone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device and usage information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored only on your device

The game saves your scores, recent runs, earned coins, unlocked skins and sound and haptic settings on your iPhone. This information is not sent to the developer. It is deleted when you delete the app. The developer does not run any game server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner and full-screen ads. To deliver, limit and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix errors, Google may collect:

InformationExamplesPurpose
Device identifiersAdvertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), identifier for vendorsThird-party advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate locationGeneral region derived from IP addressShowing ads suitable for your region, fraud prevention
Advertising and usage dataAds shown, taps on ads, app launchesThird-party advertising, analytics for ad performance
DiagnosticsCrash and performance data from the ad SDKKeeping ads working correctly

The developer does not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Tracking permission

On first launch, iOS asks whether you allow the app to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you allow it, Google may use your advertising identifier for personalized ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is not shared and you still get the full game. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are used. The legal basis for personalized advertising is your consent. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Ad privacy options.

Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your score is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard so you can compare results with other players. Apple handles this under the Apple Privacy Policy. Game Center is optional, and you can sign out in the Settings app.

Email support

If you email the developer, your email address and message are used only to answer you. They are not shared or sold, and they are deleted when they are no longer needed for your request.

What the developer does not do

  • Sell your personal information.
  • Use analytics tools other than those built into the ad SDK.
  • Offer in-app purchases or collect payment information.

Children

Drop & Balance is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information by email, contact the developer and it will be deleted.

Your rights and choices

You can limit ad tracking in iPhone Settings, change ad consent in the game, and delete all game data by deleting the app. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to its processing. Send requests to popoviclazar65@gmail.com. Requests about data held by Google or Apple can also be sent directly to them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
