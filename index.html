About the game

Drop & Balance is a physics arcade game for iPhone. Tap or hold left and right to tilt a platform while objects fall, bounce and pile up. Keep everything on the board for as long as you can.

The game is free and supported by ads. Coins are earned only by playing, and they unlock cosmetic platform styles. There are no in-app purchases.
